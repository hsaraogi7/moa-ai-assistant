# MOA Overview: Program, Ladder, Principles, Glossary

## The program
- MOA = Micro Offer Accelerator, run by Sahil Sehgal (6FA = Six Figure Accelerator, his company). [Source: MOA S1 slides]
- 4 months, built in stages: Month 1 = micro offer + funnel + ads + optimization (S1-4). Month 2 = Sweet Spot Offer + AOV stack (S5-8). Month 3 = scaling + operating system (S9-10). Month 4 = high ticket (S11-13). [Source: MOA slides]
- Core goal: daily paid buyers from cold traffic, then ascend those buyers into bigger offers. [Source: MOA S1 slides]

## Core thesis
- People still pay for info because info is not the problem, overwhelm is. They pay for STRUCTURE, SPEED (saves 100 hours of searching), ACCOUNTABILITY and TRUST. [Source: MOA S1 slides]
- Trust recession: free funnels are broken (opt-in and show-up rates are down). Free leads are tire-kickers. A paid buyer, even at $1, is far more committed. "The paid buyer era is here." [Source: MOA S1 slides]
- "Paid buyers compound. Free leads don't." "When someone pays you $7, they've already crossed a psychological line that free leads never cross." [Source: 1SAD masterclass]
- The micro offer is the buyer acquisition machine. The profit lives in what comes next (bumps, upsells, backend). [Source: 1SAD masterclass]

## The ladder (Micro-Offer Ecosystem)
Three tiers [Source: MOA S1 slides]:
1. Micro offer, $1-$97: cold traffic, builds trust fast.
2. Sweet Spot Offer (SSO), $997-$1,997: the mid-ticket bridge.
3. High Ticket (HT), $5K+: the most committed buyers ascend naturally.

Full "Million Dollar Ecosystem" map [Source: MOA S5 slides]:
- Micro ($3-$97) -> Order bump (same or 1.5x the micro) -> Upsell 1 ($97-$197) -> Upsell 2 ($297-$497) or Downsell (2-3 payments, paid trial, LITE version) -> SSO ($997-$2K) -> HT done-for-you or 1:1 ($5K+) -> Super HT inner circle / mastermind ($20K+).
- McDonald's analogy: burger = main offer, fries = order bump, meal combo = upsell. [Source: Sahil Q&A]
- Offers stack, they do not overlap. The micro offer solves problem #1. The SSO solves problem #2, the problem the buyer meets next. [Source: Micro-Offer Generator / Tool 1 pack]
- Two buyer types: Ready NOW (invite straight from the thank-you page: book a call, Google Doc offer) and Ready LATER (nurture by email and DMs, invite to an event). [Source: 1SAD masterclass]

## Build order (do not skip ahead)
- Stage 1: MVP page. Stage 2: add an order bump once the front end converts 3%+. Stage 3: upsells and the full funnel once the bump converts 20%+. [Source: MOA S2 slides]
- 90-day roadmap: Days 1-30 optimize and stabilize (first profitable funnel). Days 31-60 scale and expand (first SSO sale). Days 61-90 ascend and monetize (first HT conversation). [Source: MOA S10 slides]

## Sweet Spot Offer (SSO) essentials
- SSO = $997-$1,997. "You've built the door. Today we build what's behind it." [Source: MOA S5 slides]
- Micro buyers are already ready: they paid, trust is built, they have the next problem. You invite, not push. [Source: MOA S5 slides]
- 7 elements: right promise, benefit-driven name, delivery method, support, objection-busting bonuses, pricing, reason to act now. [Source: MOA S5 slides]
- Elevator pitch: "How to [result] in [timeframe] without [thing they don't want] so you can [dream outcome]." [Source: MOA S5 slides]
- Name the result, not the method ("Social Media Training Program" is weak, "The 30-Day Daily Paid Buyer System" is strong). [Source: MOA S5 slides]
- Bonuses must save TIME, save MONEY or make it EASIER (1:1 strategy call, swipe/script bank, done-for-you asset, bonus audit). [Source: MOA S5 slides]
- Pricing: $997 or 4x$297. $1,497 or 3x$597 / 6x$297. $1,997 or 3x$747 / 12x$197. [Source: MOA S5 slides]
- Reasons to act now must be honest: limited spots, fast-action bonus, founding price increase, discount (careful). [Source: MOA S5 slides]
- Selling it: "You're not selling, you're inviting. You're not convincing, you're continuing." Pick ONE method: DMs (1-50 buyers), sales calls (thank-you page / portal live), live event (10-100+ warm people), launch sequence (100+ buyers, list or community). [Source: MOA S6 slides]
- DM method: identify who is ready (first 5) -> start a conversation (no pitch) -> qualify (what are you working on, what's holding you back) -> send the Ugly Google Doc (who, what, outcome, price, how to join). [Source: MOA S6 slides]
- "The product is built in the delivery. You don't build it first, you sell it first." Soft launch: 1 transformation post or email -> DM 3-5 engaged micro buyers -> collect the first YES before building. [Source: MOA S7 slides]

## High ticket essentials
- HT = the next floor of the building: deeper promise, longer engagement, more access. Slides give both "$5K+" and "$3K-$10K+"; default to the 5x rule on the SSO ($997 -> $5K, $1,497 -> $7.5K, $1,997 -> $10K). [Source: MOA S11 slides]
- 4 parts: outcome (one sentence), duration (3 months minimum, 6 standard), delivery (specific), success criteria. Position outcomes, not sessions. [Source: MOA S11 slides]
- Check-in: "Hey [name], just checking in... how did you get on with [offer]? Did you get a chance to implement it? Would love to hear how it went." [Source: MOA S12 slides]
- 3 qualifying questions: main goal in the next 3-6 months, biggest obstacle, ever invested in coaching before. [Source: MOA S12 slides]
- HT Ugly Google Doc: who this is for, what we do together, what you get (5-7 lines), the investment (one line), how to start ("Reply YES and I'll send the payment link"). "The doc anchors, the DM closes." [Source: MOA S12 slides]
- Qualify before you offer: 1:1 only for people already making $5K or more. [Source: Sahil Q&A]
- "Sale is what you do FOR people, not TO people." [Source: MOA S13 slides]

## Core principles (use these as the assistant's defaults)
- Validate first, build later. "Don't fall in love with your idea. Fall in love with what your MARKET wants." [Source: MOA S2 slides]
- "You don't need a finished product, just a page and a payment link." [Source: MOA S2 slides]
- Simple scales, fancy fails. Clarity beats perfection. Done beats perfect. The first version is ugly. [Source: MOA slides, Sahil Q&A]
- Model, don't copy. "Don't be inventive, be innovative." Copy structure, never words or creatives. [Source: MOA S1 slides, 1SAD masterclass]
- Make decisions on data, not emotion. Change ONE thing at a time. [Source: MOA S3-S4 slides]
- Raise perceived value before cutting price. [Source: Tool 1 pack, Sahil Q&A]
- Consistency, not intensity. "Bad weeks are data." [Source: MOA S10 slides]
- "One offer, one funnel, one mentor. Follow something for 90 days." [Source: 1SAD masterclass]

## Operating rhythm
- Daily 15-20 min: ads Red/Yellow/Green, email stats, DMs/community, Stripe/GHL transactions. "Close the tab. Go build." [Source: MOA S10 slides]
- Weekly: Monday 5-7 new creatives + content plan. Mid-week traction review, buyer email, SSO follow-ups. Friday kill 3x-AOV losers, scale greens 20%, log CPA / AOV / bump rate / SSO leads. [Source: MOA S10 slides]
- Monthly: funnel math, ascension pipeline, creative fatigue (CPA creeping for 3-4 weeks), pick ONE focus. [Source: MOA S10 slides]
- Ascension habit: every week, 3-5 buyers who bought 2-4 weeks ago get a one-line check-in, "how did you get on with [offer]?" No pitch, no link. [Source: MOA S10 slides]
