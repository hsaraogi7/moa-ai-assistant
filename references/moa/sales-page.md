# Sales Page: Structure, Copy Rules, Mockups, Thank-You Page, Scorecard

## The MVP sales page (4 elements only)
Sahil's first-funnel standard: headline, subheadline, product mockup, button with the price. No testimonials, no case studies needed to start. [Source: MOA S2 slides, 1SAD masterclass]

Section by section, top to bottom [Source: MOA S2 slides, 15-Minute MVP Funnel doc, Sahil Q&A]:
1. Call-out line (optional, above the headline): names the ONE ideal customer.
2. Headline: the outcome, bold, at the top. Product name sits lower.
3. Subheadline: result and how fast, or the objection handled.
4. Product mockup: shows what they get. Must not push the button below the fold.
5. Button with the price on it, visible without scrolling on mobile.
6. (Lower, optional) real screenshots from inside the product to raise perceived value.
7. Checkout: two-step order form (name + email, then payment). Bump appears here once the front end converts 3%+.

- Warm-test version is even simpler: what I got / what it does / how to get it. No design, bonuses or FAQ. [Source: MOA S2 slides]
- The 15-Minute MVP Funnel builds only: Ad -> Sales Page -> Stripe or PayPal checkout -> Thank-you page. Bumps and upsells come later. "Don't build the mansion before you know anyone wants to live in it." [Source: 15-Minute MVP Funnel doc]
- Page rules: short, mobile-first, high contrast, no fake testimonials (leave [TESTIMONIAL PLACEHOLDER]), Stripe as the main button, PayPal secondary. [Source: 15-Minute MVP Funnel doc]

## Headlines
- Slide formula: "Discover How To [RESULT] Using [METHOD] In [TIME FRAME] Or Less." Under 12 words, specific result, never start with your name. [Source: MOA S2 slides]
- Q&A variants: "Discover how to [RESULT] using [METHOD] in [TIMEFRAME] without [objection]." "How to [X] without [Y]." [Source: Sahil Q&A]
- Generator variants: "[Result] without [thing they don't want]." "How to [X] in [timeframe] (even if [objection])." "The [number]-step [system] that [benefit]." [Source: Micro-Offer Generator]
- Outcome first, then the product name. Add a timeframe ("next 7 days", "over the weekend"; weekend works for people with jobs). [Source: Sahil Q&A]
- Vague title fix: "Multifamily Quick Start Guide" -> "How to analyze your first multifamily deal in 30 minutes, so you buy on numbers and not on hype." [Source: Sahil Q&A]
- Outsider test: if you met someone and they asked what you do, the one line you'd say is the hook. [Source: Sahil Q&A]
- Use the audience's exact words. Funnel-hack first: study proven funnels, copy the headline STRUCTURE, not the words. [Source: Sahil Q&A]
- Vague headline is the #1 fix Sahil makes in reviews. The headline is always tested first. [Source: Session summaries, MOA S4 slides]

## Subheadline
Does ONE of: adds specificity, addresses the objection, stacks the outcome. [Source: MOA S2 slides]
MVP doc version: the result and how fast. [Source: 15-Minute MVP Funnel doc]

## Buttons (CTA)
- Big, high contrast, action verbs, price in the button, first person converts better ("Get My Toolkit for $17"). Never "Submit" or "Click Here". [Source: MOA S2 slides]
- Visible without scrolling on mobile. Check phone and tablet. [Source: 15-Minute MVP Funnel doc, Sahil Q&A]
- Test every button yourself with a real test purchase before running ads. [Source: Sahil Q&A]

## VSL and longer pages
- Under about $50: no VSL or lead magnet needed. Near $100+: a VSL or a 10-12 page doc helps build trust. [Source: Sahil Q&A]
- Hybrid works: VSL page for watchers plus a written doc for readers. [Source: Sahil Q&A]
- Sahil's VSL rule: the buy button only appears after about 7 minutes of watching, so people actually watch. [Source: Sahil Q&A]
- Selling an SSO live: drop a checkout page or Google Doc, not a full sales page. [Source: MOA S6 slides]

## Mockups (product images)
What Sahil's material says:
- The mockup must exist, not be perfect. Make it with AI image gen, Canva, screenshots or a phone. [Source: MOA S2 slides]
- MVP doc spec: premium product mockup, transparent background, looks "worth at least 10x the price", uses the brand colors. [Source: 15-Minute MVP Funnel doc]
- Too big = button pushed below the fold. "If I'm seeing it, I should be able to see a button at least, without scrolling." [Source: Sahil Q&A]
- Prefer real screenshots from inside the product (diagrams, worksheets, portal) over AI mockups. "Everyone knows AI-generated is AI. It does not carry value. Real screenshots create curiosity." [Source: Sahil Q&A]
- Add more real screenshots lower on the page to raise perceived value. [Source: Sahil Q&A]
- Don't rely only on AI. Use the Canva templates Sahil provides. [Source: Sahil Q&A]
- Order bumps need a mockup too, so the bump looks complete. [Source: Sahil Q&A]
- Ads can use a mockup style (AI Ad mode). See ads.md. [Source: SPK Method doc]

Assistant brief template for a mockup (built only from the rules above):
- What to show: the actual deliverable (cover + 2-3 inner pages / screens), real screenshots where they exist.
- Background: transparent PNG.
- Colors: member's brand colors.
- Feel: premium, worth 10x the price, never cluttered.
- Size on page: small enough that headline + mockup + button fit on one phone screen.
- Never show fake results, fake testimonials or fake income numbers in a mockup.

## Thank-you / delivery page
- Headline pattern: "You're In! Your [PRODUCT] Is On Its Way." 2-3 next steps, a soft CTA, Meta pixel Purchase event on page load. [Source: 15-Minute MVP Funnel doc]
- Best simple next steps: 1) access your product, 2) join the community, 3) book your audit call (free 15-minute audit as a bonus, "only if you book in the next 30 days"). [Source: Sahil Q&A]
- Passive SSO sales: a booking CTA on the thank-you page ("Want to go deeper?") and a "What's Next" area in the membership portal. [Source: MOA S6 slides]
- Ready Now buyers can be invited straight from the thank-you page (call or Google Doc offer). [Source: 1SAD masterclass]
- Service providers wanting appointments: micro offer -> thank-you page application or call. [Source: Sahil Q&A]

## Funnel and tracking rules that affect the page
- Two-step order form (name + email first, then payment) so you see opt-ins and can run abandoned cart. A 1-step form hides opt-ins and makes tests inconclusive. [Source: MOA S2 slides, Sahil Q&A]
- Never send organic and paid traffic to the same funnel. Clone the whole funnel per source. [Source: Sahil Q&A]
- In GHL, the order form button should go to the thank-you page (payment processed in GHL), not to a separate Stripe page. [Source: Sahil Q&A]
- Test mode: turn live payments off, use test card 4242 4242 4242 4242, confirm delivery and tagging fire. Lead tag on opt-in; Page View, Lead and Purchase pixel events must all fire. [Source: Sahil Q&A]
- Stripe (or PayPal) is needed for one-click upsells. [Source: Sahil Q&A]
- AI-built pages in GHL: use a custom HTML element, split code into sections for several buttons or pop-ups, fix white space via section background color and smaller headline font. [Source: Sahil Q&A]
- Post the funnel in the community for feedback before spending on ads. [Source: Sahil Q&A]

## Fix order when the page is not converting
- Need 100 unique views minimum (150 better) before any conclusion. [Source: Sahil Q&A]
- If no sales after 100 views: headline -> price -> button visible -> who it calls out -> funnel flow. [Source: Sahil Q&A]
- Split-test order: headline -> price -> bump -> upsell -> button -> layout. One variable, full week, 100+ visitors per version. [Source: MOA S4 slides]
- Good CTR but low CVR: rewrite the headline, check the CTA, make the price visible. [Source: MOA S3 slides]
- Clicks but zero opt-ins or sales: suspect pixel or page, not the ad. [Source: Sahil Q&A]
- Log the date of every change. [Source: Sahil Q&A]

## Sales page scorecard
Rate each Green / Yellow / Red / Info (Info = can't see it, e.g. a mockup from page text only). [Source: Action Hub prompts]
| Row | Green when |
|---|---|
| Ideal customer called out | One specific person, in their words |
| Headline | Outcome first, timeframe, under 12 words, not starting with a name |
| Subheadline | Adds specificity, handles the objection, or stacks the outcome |
| Product mockup | Present, real screenshots preferred, not pushing the button down |
| Button and price | Action verb, first person, price on it, visible without scrolling |
| Mobile | Readable without zoom, thumb-sized buttons |
| Checkout | Two-step order form, test purchase done, pixel events firing |

Output pattern: what's working first, the ONE fix in Sahil's order, a rewritten headline in his formula, how many views to wait. Under 100 unique views = no big changes. Writing a new page: headline + 2 alternates, subheadline, mockup brief, button text with price; next move = real test purchase before ads. [Source: Action Hub prompts]

## Common mistakes
- Vague headline, no timeframe, name or product first. [Source: Sahil Q&A]
- Mockup too big, button below the fold. [Source: Sahil Q&A]
- AI-only visuals with no real screenshots. [Source: Sahil Q&A]
- 1-step order form (no abandoned cart, no opt-in data). [Source: Sahil Q&A]
- Fake testimonials. [Source: 15-Minute MVP Funnel doc]
- Building bonuses, FAQ, long copy before the offer is validated. [Source: MOA S2 slides]
- Judging the page on 20-30 clicks. [Source: Sahil Q&A]
- Changing two things at once. [Source: Sahil Q&A]
