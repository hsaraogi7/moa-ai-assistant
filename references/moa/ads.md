# Ads: SPK, Angles, Image Ads, Testing, Scorecard

## Mindset
- Ads are not gambling. $20 a day = real data. "Organic builds trust. Paid builds volume." [Source: MOA S3 slides]
- Early ad spend is an investment in data. [Source: Session summaries]
- 4 truths: creative does 80% of the work; go broad and trust the algorithm; a fresh pixel is in learning, don't kill in 2-3 days; ugly ads beat fancy ads. [Source: MOA S3 slides]
- Messaging matters more than production. People buy the offer, not the person, so faceless ads can work for micro offers. [Source: Sahil Q&A, 1SAD masterclass]

## SPK Method (ad copy) [Source: SPK Method doc]
Every winning cold-traffic ad does three things, in order:
- S = Stop line: 3-5 words, grabs attention, the BIGGEST text.
- P = Pull line: 6-8 words, curiosity or emotion.
- K = Kill line: 5-7 words, removes the objection, often includes the price.
- "Most people only nail the Stop. That's why they get views but no clicks."
- Rules: max 10 words per line, price in every variation, write for strangers, punchy, direct, no corporate language.
- Inputs needed: product name, price, main result, ideal buyer, their #1 frustration, proof (real only).

## The 5 angles (one copy set each) [Source: SPK Method doc]
1. RESULT: the specific outcome.
2. PAIN: their #1 frustration.
3. PRICE / VALUE: what they get vs the low price.
4. CURIOSITY: contrarian take.
5. PROOF: a real result number. If the member has none, write it around a specific outcome and mark it [NEEDS YOUR REAL NUMBER]. Never invent proof. [Source: Action Hub prompts]
- Awareness stage also shapes the angle: problem-aware = call out pain, solution-aware = why your approach is different, product-aware = urgency + proof. [Source: MOA S1 slides]
- Pain buyers respond to "Stop X in Y time", desire buyers to "Become Z by Y time". [Source: MOA S1 slides]

## Ad types [Source: MOA S3 slides]
- Ugly (most used), AI (fastest), Meme (engagement), Lifestyle (trust), B-roll / Reel (reach). Start with Ugly or AI.
- Test one creative type per week (images, then video, then meme, then lifestyle). [Source: Sahil Q&A]
- Image ads are enough under about $50. Offers near $97+ need video and trust. Repurpose a winning organic reel as an ad. [Source: Sahil Q&A]
- Simple images beat video 2-3x on CPA for low ticket. [Source: MOA S3 slides]

## Image ad rules
- Size: square, 1200x1200 or 1000x1000. [Source: MOA S3 slides]
- High contrast pairs: black + yellow, red + white, blue + white, dark + orange. [Source: MOA S3 slides]
- Split: image copy 80%, ad (primary text) copy 20%. [Source: MOA S3 slides]
- 2-second rule: lead with the benefit, one message, high contrast, include the price. [Source: MOA S3 slides]
- Three image modes [Source: SPK Method doc]:
  1. Reference-based: attach a winning ad, recreate it for your offer, 1:1, price visible. (Model, don't copy.)
  2. Ugly Ad: solid dark background, bold text, yellow / orange / white / green accents, Stop line biggest, price prominent, a CTA button, no faces, no stock photos, 1200x1200.
  3. AI Ad: comparison split (red vs green) or mockup style, dark background, 2 accent colors, CTA at the bottom.
- Generate 2-3 versions and pick the best. [Source: SPK Method doc]
- Sources of proven formats: Facebook Ads Library (running 3-6+ months = winner), Sahil's Winning Ad Creatives Vault. Use AI to analyze and adapt the format, never copy words or creative. [Source: MOA S1 slides, Session summaries]

Image prompt checklist the assistant should fill:
- Mode (reference / ugly / AI), 1:1 size, background, 2 accent colors.
- Stop line (biggest), Pull line, Kill line with price, CTA button text.
- Product mockup only if it's the member's real product (see sales-page.md Mockups).
- No fake screenshots, no fake income, no stock faces.

## Campaign setup (first launch)
- 1 campaign, Sales objective only -> 1-2 ad sets at $20 a day, broad -> 5-7 creatives. [Source: MOA S3 slides]
- Top 6 countries first (US, UK, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Ireland). Worldwide = cheaper clicks but more failed payments, refunds and spam; exclude weak countries if you go wide. [Source: Sahil Q&A]
- ABO over CBO. Don't mix playbooks. [Source: Sahil Q&A]
- With $20 a day, 5-7 creatives (even 3). 20-30 creatives only with a big budget. [Source: Sahil Q&A]
- Match the ad account's currency. [Source: Sahil Q&A]
- Enable columns: unique outbound clicks, unique outbound CTR, landing page views. [Source: Sahil Q&A]
- Before launching: every button tested with a real purchase, pixel firing Page View / Lead / Purchase. [Source: Sahil Q&A]

## Testing rules
- Learning phase: Days 1-3 don't touch. Days 4-7 look for patterns. Day 7+ decide. Run 5-7 days minimum. [Source: MOA S3 slides]
- Validation budget: $100-$150 over 7 days for a micro offer. [Source: Sahil Q&A]
- Under 100 unique clicks: change nothing. 20-30 clicks tell you nothing. [Source: Sahil Q&A]
- Change ONE thing. Low CTR -> change the image first, then the first line. [Source: MOA S3 slides]
- Angle test: keep primary text, headline and description constant so only the tested variable changes. Test 5 copy variations of a winning format in a new ad set at $15-$20 a day. [Source: Session summaries]
- 3x kill switch: spent 3x AOV with zero purchases -> pause. [Source: MOA S4 slides]
- Weekly: new creatives Monday, kill / scale Friday, always one split test running. [Source: MOA S4 slides]

## Scaling
- Winning ad = minimum 3 purchases AND CPA at or below target. Both, or it doesn't graduate. [Source: MOA S9 slides]
- Two campaigns: Testing ($10-$20 per ad set per day; broad countries-only, then 1-2 interest sets, then lookalikes once there's pixel data) and Scaling ASC ($50-$100 a day, proven creatives only, countries only, zero targeting). "Test cheap. Graduate 3+ winners. Scale with ASC." [Source: MOA S9 slides]
- Graduate winners via Post ID to keep social proof. [Source: MOA S4 slides]
- 20% rule: raise budget max 20% per 48 hours ($50 -> $60, not $100). [Source: MOA S9 slides]
- Multiplication Method: image -> video, same creative different colors, same message different hook, same hook different visual. [Source: MOA S4 slides]
- Lookalikes: source quality is everything (100-200+ purchases). Ranked sources: purchasers, website visitors 90d, 75% video viewers, Facebook engagers, Instagram engagers, customer list. First LLA = 1% purchasers (visitors if under 50 purchases). [Source: MOA S9 slides]
- Creative fatigue: CPA creeping up for 3-4 weeks. [Source: MOA S10 slides]
- Q4: costs rise, be ready to double budget ($20 -> $40 a day) and decide with data. [Source: Sahil Q&A]

## Claims and compliance
- Never invent results or income claims for a member. Proof must be the member's real, provable number. [Assistant rule, from README]
- Sahil has run results-style claims in his own ads; members must use their own judgment and follow Meta's ad policies. Have a visible privacy policy and terms on any page collecting personal and payment data. [Source: Sahil Q&A, Sahil's own funnel setup]

## Ad scorecard
Rate Green / Yellow / Red, then ONE fix and a rewrite. [Source: Action Hub prompts]
| Row | Green when |
|---|---|
| Stop | Grabs in 3-5 words, biggest text |
| Pull | Curiosity or emotion in 6-8 words |
| Kill | Removes the objection, shows the price |
| Looks like an ad for strangers | Benefit first, no insider language, one message |
| Visual | High contrast, square, readable in 2 seconds, price visible |
| Angle fit | Matches pain or desire buyer and awareness stage |

Output pattern for "write my ads": 5 SPK copy sets (one per angle, labelled), 1 Ugly Ad image prompt, launch settings (Sales objective, $20 a day, broad, top 6 countries, 5-7 creatives, run 5-7 days), next move = upload and publish. [Source: Action Hub prompts]
